# Manufacturing Features Presentation Script

## Feature 1: Advanced Manufacturing Overview Dashlet
**Location**: Home Dashboard

**Script**: 
"This is our Manufacturing Overview Dashlet that gives you a real-time snapshot of your entire manufacturing operation. You can see production status, inventory levels, and output rates all in one place with interactive charts. Simply click on any chart element to drill down for more details, and use the filters to focus on specific time periods or production lines. It's like having a manufacturing control tower right on your dashboard."

**Key Points**: Real-time metrics, interactive charts, drill-down capabilities, centralized overview

---

## Feature 2: Enhanced Production Tracking in Opportunities Module
**Location**: Opportunities Module

**Script**:
"We've transformed the Opportunities module into a comprehensive production management hub. When you open any opportunity, you'll see dedicated panels for Production Planning, Risk Assessment, and Capacity Planning. The system automatically tracks resource allocation, manages production timelines with dependencies, and calculates risk assessments. It even updates opportunity stages automatically as production milestones are reached through built-in workflows."

**Key Points**: Production planning integration, automated risk assessment, capacity planning, workflow automation

---

## Feature 3: Inventory Monitoring and Threshold Notifications
**Location**: Products Module (AOS_Products)

**Script**:
"Our smart inventory monitoring system keeps track of stock levels automatically. You simply set your reorder points and threshold levels for each product, and the system continuously monitors your inventory. When stock gets low, you'll see color-coded alerts - green means you're good, yellow means pay attention, and red means take action now. The system even calculates reorder points and forecasts demand based on your historical data."

**Key Points**: Automated monitoring, color-coded alerts, smart reorder calculations, demand forecasting

---

## Feature 4: Comprehensive Quality Control Subpanel
**Location**: Cases Module

**Script**:
"Quality control is now fully integrated into our Cases module. You can track batch information, categorize defects, set severity levels, and maintain complete audit trails. The system automatically logs all changes and links quality issues to specific products. Plus, we have built-in trend analysis reports that generate on-the-fly, so you can spot quality patterns before they become bigger problems."

**Key Points**: Batch tracking, defect categorization, automatic audit trails, trend analysis reports

---

## Feature 5: Detailed Supplier Performance Analytics
**Location**: Quotes Module (AOS_Quotes)

**Script**:
"Supplier performance tracking is now visual and automated. In any quote, you can see interactive charts showing supplier performance ratings, quality scores, and delivery timelines. The system tracks delivery variance, cost trends, and defect rates automatically. We have pre-built reports for supplier analysis, and the system even creates alerts when supplier performance drops below acceptable levels."

**Key Points**: Visual performance tracking, automated metrics calculation, interactive charts, performance alerts

---

## Feature 6: Internal Manufacturing KPI Calculator
**Location**: Reports Module (AOR_Reports)

**Script**:
"This is our Manufacturing KPI Calculator - think of it as your manufacturing analytics engine. You can calculate 8 different types of KPIs including efficiency ratios, yield predictions, bottleneck analysis, and overall equipment effectiveness. Simply select your KPI type, set your calculation period and targets, and the system does the heavy lifting. Results are color-coded so you can quickly see what's performing well and what needs attention."

**Key Points**: 8 KPI calculation types, configurable parameters, color-coded results, comprehensive analytics
